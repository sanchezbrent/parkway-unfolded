# Sources and reuse

This is an independent visualization project. It is not an official NJDOT or
NJ Turnpike Authority service. Source references are preserved in
`data/Source_References.json` and the application.

No repository-wide reuse license has been selected. Public repository access
does not grant a blanket license to the code, artwork or third-party data.
Source datasets, linked materials and marks retain their applicable terms.
Contact the repository owner before reusing material beyond those terms.

The project does not include the statewide raw roadway archive. Its official
download URL and retained snapshot hash are documented in the source references;
the selected Parkway coordinates and derived exports are included.
