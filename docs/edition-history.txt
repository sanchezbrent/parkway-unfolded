PARKWAY UNFOLDED — COMPLETE PARKWAY ATLAS
Updated October 23, 2026

Open GSP_Parkway_County_Refined.html in a browser. It is one self-contained
offline page: overview, interchanges, shape collection, comparison and Top 10s.
No separate pages, external fonts, scripts or data downloads are required.

CHANGES
One integrated Traffic/Tolls toggle group in the navigation; toll badges on
54 selected shapes associated with a listed charged movement; the prior toll
anatomy illustration is removed. Top 10s has 15 categories: 11 shape and 4 traffic comparisons.
County scope is shared across the atlas. Toll-price superlatives are removed.
Traffic includes most/least positive AADT and busiest/quietest tolled movements.
Equal values share a rank. Ten rows are shown even if a tie crosses the cutoff.
Toll pricing remains available in the map layer and directory.

PRICING
The directory and location details show cash (all times), NJ E-ZPass peak and
NJ E-ZPass off-peak together. Ten vehicle/plan profiles use official 2026 fares.
Standard Class 1 passenger-car Parkway fares are the same at peak and off-peak.
Eligible NJ E-ZPass truck/bus and enrolled Senior/Green Pass discounts differ.
Peak is weekdays 7–9 a.m. and 4:30–6:30 p.m., plus all Saturday/Sunday (Eastern).
Heavy trucks are prohibited north of interchange 105. Sayreville does not
accept cash. Trailer and truck-volume plans are not applied. See the official
source schedules and eligibility links in each location's source disclosure.

SCOPE
359 selected paths at 84 interchange groups; 12,877 original vertices remain.
Geometry: stored NJDOT snapshot, August 21, 2025. Traffic: FHWA HPMS 2024 annual
average vehicles/day; 277 paths have positive linked estimates, 82 unavailable.
47 of the 54 paths associated with a tolled movement have usable traffic data.
These are source-road estimates, not unique people, toll transactions or live
congestion. Counts on shared roads are not added. The toll directory has 34 named tariff entries; these are not a count of
physical booths or entire trips.
Map toll markers are approximate references; booth/lane counts are unavailable.

SIGNAGE — HELD
Public NJTA inspection material describes sign structures and an AssetWise
database, but the sources reviewed did not provide a complete, current,
publicly usable sign-panel text/legend inventory for character-count rankings.
No character-count feature is included. A verified sign-text export, with
panel IDs, wording, date and coverage, would be needed to add it reliably.
Research: NJTA bridge/sign inspection notifications; OPS P3937/P3938 sign
structure inspection scope; current sign design standards. Checked Oct 22, 2026.

FILES
The complete HTML is the only file needed to use the atlas. JSON/CSV/GeoJSON
files provide inspectable source data and matches. Pricing CSV has 340 profile
and tariff combinations. Default Top 10 CSV has the 15 shape/traffic categories. Interactive
ranking exports use the current stretch of road and movement; pricing exports
use the directory’s selected vehicle profile.
Source/rebuild_page.py rebuilds the same HTML from the included template and
editable modules. Verification JSON files record the actual browser/data checks.

Navigation refinement: icons on all five workspace buttons, gold Top 10s emphasis, and Stretch of road scope label. Project title: Parkway Unfolded.

EDITORIAL EDITION — OCTOBER 22, 2026
The public copy addresses everyday Parkway drivers. Technical terms have been
replaced or explained in navigation, ramp details, rankings, traffic and tolls.
Three optional starting points introduce the unfolding view, a 2.2-mile shared
connection at Exit 129, and the looping shapes. Long source/verification notes
are collapsed under About the data. Original records remain available there
and inside each drawing’s data notes. Dates, partial drawings, missing traffic
estimates and uncertain labels remain explicit.

Straightest stretches displays percent extra distance over a straight line.
Ranking CSVs export that percentage plus separate raw-value/raw-unit columns.
All original coordinates, raw measurements, traffic counts and toll prices are
retained. Editorial_Verification.json records this edition’s browser checks.
Screenshots used for review are not part of the deliverable.

VISUAL EDITION — OCTOBER 23, 2026
White and charcoal surfaces replace the warm palette. All headings use Arial /
Helvetica. Supporting text and controls are at least 14 px; inputs stay 16 px
on small screens. Flat navigation and plain section rules replace nested frames,
rounded cards, inset accents and decorative effects. Gold keeps Top 10s distinct.
Green identifies the Parkway and road signs; neutral fills show selected controls.
The System / Light / Dark control defaults to the system preference, follows
system changes and remembers an explicit choice. Map surfaces and drawings use
the same theme. The Top 10 hero drawing adapts to its actual display width so
scale labels remain readable. Public editorial copy and all data are retained.
Source/visual_styles.css and Source/visual_module.js own the visual refinement;
rebuild_page.py includes them. Visual_Verification.json documents this edition.

TITLE LETTERING — OCTOBER 23, 2026
The P in Parkway is a looped ramp; the U in Unfolded is a hairpin curve.
They use the amber/teal drawing colors, with theme-aware contrast. These are
decorative letterforms; no road data or rankings changed. The full heading
name remains accessible. Assets/Parkway_Unfolded_Wordmark.svg is a reusable
vector title; the PNG is a white-background preview. Title_Verification.json
records the focused checks. Source/rebuild_page.py reproduces the HTML.

MASTHEAD LOGO
The stylized Parkway Unfolded wordmark is now the sole logo at the top of
the page. The preceding stacked GSP badge is removed.
