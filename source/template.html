Map line system

Widths are graphic treatments, not lane count or pavement width. Use only supplied line geometry. No invented trees, building footprints, interchange shields or satellite basemaps.

Geometry & motion

Retain all original XY coordinates. Translation and uniform scale only; north stays up. The fitted comparison uses individual scales. The same-scale comparison uses equal screen pixels per source foot, including unequal screen widths.

Motion follows a recorded branch; it does not simulate traffic or imply a continuous driving route. The source inspector offers a manual trace. Respect reduced-motion preferences and pause controls.

Maximum-curvature classifications and “gentle / tight” engineering labels from the concept illustrations are not implemented: they are not source attributes.

About Parkway Unfolded

Parkway Unfolded explores the Garden State Parkway through the shapes of its entrance ramps, exit ramps and connecting roads. It pulls those roads out of the map so you can look closely at their loops, bends and connections.

What you’re looking at

Each line traces a real stretch of road from New Jersey’s road map. There are 359 drawings here: some show a whole ramp, while others show a fork or part of a shared connection. When the roads move off the map, they keep their bends and proportions. North stays up.

Where to begin

  1. Search for your exit or choose a county on the Parkway map.
  2. Open Explore exits to see how the ramps connect to nearby roads.
  3. Try Ramp shapes to pull the roads apart, Top 10s to browse rankings, or Compare two ramps to look at two drawings side by side.

Adopt a favorite

Meet a random ramp, or choose one while you explore. Give it a nickname, save its adoption paper, and revisit it in My ramps. Your collection stays in this browser. The road names are real; the adoption is just for fun.

More than shapes

Turn on Traffic for average daily vehicle counts from 2024, or Tolls for published 2026 rates. The moving dots help you follow a road’s curves; they do not show cars or traffic conditions right now.

About the data

Road shapes come from NJDOT’s August 2025 map. Traffic is based on 2024 daily averages. Toll prices use NJTA’s 2026 schedules.

Sources and coverage

These drawings show 359 ramps and parts of connecting roads. Some show only part of your drive. North stays up, and each shape keeps its proportions.

Traffic estimates are available for 277 drawings. Some ramps share a road count. The numbers do not show traffic right now, and a missing estimate does not mean an empty road.

Toll markers show approximate locations. Choose a toll to see prices and which direction pays.
